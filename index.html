Research Interests and Publications

Die Ros' ist ohn' Warum; sie blühet, weil sie blühet
Sie acht' nicht ihrer selbst, fragt nicht, ob man sie siehet.
(Silesius, 1657)

Operations research is my field of activity. My research is mainly concerned with combinatorial algorithms with applications to scheduling, logistics, manufacturing and transportation systems.

My work includes the design and analysis of decision models for resource allocation, the characterisation of computational complexity, and the design of exact and approximate algorithms for multi-agent and nonlinear combinatorial optimization problems. I also design and implement simulation models for evaluating control policies in complex systems. An updated list is available on my publications page.

Bio

  • Feb. 2025– Full Professor, Dipartimento di Ingegneria Civile e Ingegneria Informatica, Università di Roma Tor Vergata.
  • Dec. 2010 – Jan. 2025 Associate Professor, Dipartimento di Ingegneria Civile e Ingegneria Informatica (until May 2012, Dip. di Informatica, Sistemi e Produzione), Università di Roma Tor Vergata.
  • Nov. 2003 – Dec. 2010 Assistant Professor, Dipartimento di Ingegneria dell'Impresa, Università di Roma Tor Vergata.
  • Nov. 1999 – Nov. 2003 Assistant Professor, Dipartimento di Informatica, Sistemi e Produzione, Università di Roma Tor Vergata.
  • May–Jul. 2004 Visiting Faculty, ATLAS Research Center, The University of Arizona, Tucson, Arizona.
  • 1997–2000 Research Fellow, Dipartimento di Informatica e Automazione, Università degli Studi Roma Tre.
  • Apr.–Jul. 1997 Visiting Scholar, System and Industrial Engineering Dept., The University of Arizona, Tucson, Arizona (supervisor: Prof. Pitu B. Mirchandani).
  • 1996 Research Fellow, CNR (National Research Council).
  • 1993–1996 PhD program in Operations Research, Università di Roma La Sapienza (degree awarded July 1997).
  • 1993 "Laurea" in Electronic Engineering, with full marks, Università di Roma La Sapienza.
